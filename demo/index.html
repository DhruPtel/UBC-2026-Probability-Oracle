      What the yellow labels mean

      • PAPER TRADE The agent's insurance buy is recorded at Gemini's real price, but no order is sent. The premium it pays is real testnet USDC.
      • DECLARED HOLDING The agent's BTC is a stated amount, not a wallet balance.
      • DEMO The lending pool holds no deposits. It only works out its limit.
      • TEST A second pool on a made-up market, for showing level changes on demand.
      • REPLAY Real Gemini history, played again faster under its own symbol. Buys are priced at the replayed chance.
      • LIVE ADVISOR Read-only advice about a real protocol. Changing it takes its governance or approved risk stewards.
