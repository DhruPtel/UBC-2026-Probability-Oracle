BTC pools

The first card is our own demo pool on Arc: the risk switch sets its loan limit onchain from the oracle, and its view replays a real week of prices. The rule has three steps; the last is 85%+: no new borrowing beyond today’s level, while open loans, repayments, deposits and withdrawals carry on. The others are real lending markets that take BTC as collateral, read live every 60 seconds. There our oracle’s chance that BTC ends below a level becomes a suggested limit, in the way each protocol allows, and nothing is sent to any of them. The picture compares the BTC deposited, the most the market can lend against it today, and the most under our suggestion. The slider asks “what if the chance were this?” and every card answers. “Play the week” moves the slider through the market’s real last 7 days in about 20 seconds. Open a pool to change the market it follows: the same one as this page, the automatic pick (the strike nearest 10% below today’s BTC price among the markets that end last), or any other BTC market from the oracle.
