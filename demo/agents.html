Agents

    Live. An agent signs up with what it holds and gets an Arc testnet wallet with test USDC. It pays $0.001 over x402 for each odds read, compares Gemini’s live contracts that match its holding, and buys cover: each contract pays $1 if the bad outcome happens. The premium is real testnet USDC, paid onchain through ProtectionBook.

    The holding is declared, not held. The order to Gemini is a ticket: the exact body for Gemini’s order API at its live price, not sent, because that needs a funded US account. The same order goes to Gemini’s sandbox, with test funds, when the sandbox lists the contract. Demo wallets are a pool of four, reused by one agent after another.

    Replay. Real Gemini hourly prices for “BTC $67,500 or above”, Nov 12 to 15, posted again in about 30 seconds under a replay symbol. The reads and the premium are real testnet payments; the cover is a paper trade at the replayed price.

    Steps arrive in bursts, so the page shows them one at a time, about 3 seconds each. Gemini’s fee is its published taker fee: 0.07 × contracts × price × (1 − price), rounded up to the next cent.

    Any MCP client can use the desk. For Claude Code, run this once, then ask Claude to insure a BTC or ETH holding. Your agent appears here as “Your agent”.
