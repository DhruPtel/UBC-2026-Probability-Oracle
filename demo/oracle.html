Our oracleGemini YES

The oracle reads each market’s best bid and ask on Gemini and takes the middle as the chance of YES. Where one outcome is clearly bad for a holder (a price ending below a level, a rate hike), it posts the chance of that outcome: for an “or above” price market that is 100% minus YES. Otherwise it posts the chance of YES, not flipped. Nine markets are posted every 5 minutes; the others when their value moves by half a point or more, and once an hour, within a fixed budget of posts. Each dot on a chart is one post on Arc. The result beside a chart is what the agent’s rule or the demo pool’s tiers do at that number.
