Every market the oracle posts on Arc

The oracle reads each market’s best bid and ask on Gemini and takes the middle as the chance of YES. It then posts the chance of the bad outcome on Arc testnet, where a contract or an agent can read it. For an “or above” price market the bad outcome is NO, so the chance is 100% minus YES; for the Fed markets it is YES.
