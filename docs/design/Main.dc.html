What each user does with the BTC number (71%), and why

The oracle only posts the number. Each user picks its own rule for what to do with it.

Insurance agent

Covered

An AI agent holding $65 of BTC (0.001 BTC). It wants to be paid back if BTC falls below $67,500.

Its rule At 50% it buys all the cover it needs, in one go. Higher odds don’t make it buy more.
At 71%
Holds 7 NO contracts
Bought when the chance passed 50%
If BTC ends below $67,500 on Nov 30
Expected loss$6.50 Cover pays$7.00

Why 7: when Gemini settles the market, each NO contract pays $1 if BTC is below $67,500 and $0 if not. The agent expects a 10% hit to its $65, so 7 contracts cover it. It covers a Fed hike the same way: 4 contracts.

Lending pool

Lends 40%

A demo lending pool on Arc that lends against BTC. It chose to follow this market.

Its rule: how much it lends per $100 of BTC The higher the chance of a drop, the less it lends. At 85% it stops new loans.
At 71%
Lends up to $40 per $100
Normally $75. Changed seconds after the post.
Borrowing against $100 of BTC
Normally$75 Now$40

Why lower: a loan is safe while the BTC behind it is worth more than the loan. At $75, a 25% fall in BTC wipes out that margin. At $40 it takes a 60% fall. When the chance falls again, the limit goes back up on its own.
