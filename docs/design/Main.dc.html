The agent holds $65 of BTC and wants its money back if BTC ends below $67,500 on Nov 30. Its rule is a simple yes or no: once that chance reaches 50%, it buys cover, one time. The cover is 7 contracts on the NO side of the Gemini market, and each pays $1 if BTC ends below $67,500. That’s $7 back against the $6.50 it expects to lose. For now it is a paper trade at Gemini’s live price, with the premium paid in USDC on Arc.

Our demo pool on Arc lends against BTC and follows the oracle on its own. Every time someone takes out a loan, it checks its loan limit: at 71%, you can borrow at most $40 for each $100 of BTC, instead of the usual $75. The limit is only checked when you borrow, so loans that are already open are left alone and nobody is liquidated by it. When the odds fall, the limit goes back up.

Aave V4 is a real lending market on Arc mainnet. In V4, one setting, the collateral factor, decides both how much you can borrow and when you get liquidated: at 78%, $100 of cirBTC can back up to $78 of debt. Cutting that the old way would push open loans toward liquidation, so V4 applies a change only to new positions. An open loan keeps 78% unless its owner borrows more or withdraws collateral. We suggest 53% for new positions, and Aave’s governance decides.
