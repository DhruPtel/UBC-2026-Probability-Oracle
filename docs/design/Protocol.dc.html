What the loan limit is

The most you can borrow against what you deposit. At 75%, $100 of ETH lets you borrow up to $75.

If ETH falls, a loan can end up worth more than its collateral. So as the chance of a drop rises, the pool lowers the limit. Pausing new loans is the last step.

Chance of a drop Loan limit With $100 of ETH
Under 50%75%borrow $75
50% or more60%borrow $60
70% or more40%borrow $40

Each pool picks its own tiers once, when it joins the risk switch. In this demo pool only new loans are checked.
