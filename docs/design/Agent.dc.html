Agent insurance demo

Four real days of Gemini prices (Nov 12 to 15), replayed in about a minute. An AI agent holds $32 of BTC and insures it on its own.

Chance BTC ends below $67,500 on Nov 30

From Gemini prices, as the oracle posted it Agent’s trigger

How the agent decides

  1. 1 It holds 0.0005 BTC, worth $32.
  2. 2 If BTC ends below $67,500, it expects to lose about 10%: $3.20.
  3. 3 A NO contract pays $1 if that happens, so 4 contracts cover the loss.
  4. 4 When the chance reaches 50%, it buys them at Gemini’s price: 4 × $0.50 = $2.00.
Result: it pays $2.00 now to get $4.00 back if BTC drops, more than the $3.20 it expects to lose.

Prices are real Gemini prices. The trade is a paper trade at that price; the $2.00 premium is paid in real testnet USDC on Arc. Each read of the oracle costs $0.001.
